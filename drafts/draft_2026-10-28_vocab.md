## Check this first

1. **The chapter tag is my inference.** You didn't name it, but the words match Bāb al-Istiqāmah: قَارِبُوا وَسَدِّدُوا
   is ḥadīth 86, and al-Nawawī's notes there define istiqāmah as لُزُومُ طَاعَةِ اللهِ (holding to obedience) and
   نِظَامُ الأُمُورِ (the order of affairs). So the list is tagged `riyad-istiqamah`.
2. **I read تغشاني as تَغَشَّانِي** (Form V), "it came over me". If it's تَغْشَانِي (Form I), "it covers me", tell me.
