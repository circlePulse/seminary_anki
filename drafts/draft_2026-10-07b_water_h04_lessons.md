## Check this first

1. **The two abortion stages look swapped in your notes.** Under *before the soul enters (before 120 days)*,
   you have "totally unlawful and tantamount to murder", plus the exception for the mother's life. Under
   *after the soul enters (after 120 days)*, you have "the sin will not be murder, but violating the rights of
   one's own organs".
   That can't be right as written: killing before there is a soul can't be murder while killing after it is
   not. Mufti Muhammad ibn Adam's fatwa has them the other way round. After ensoulment, abortion is
   tantamount to murder, with the mother's-life exception. Before it, abortion is still unlawful, because the
   fetus is a trust like one's organs, but it is not murder, and rape, danger to health and so on are
   exceptions. **The cards follow the fatwa**, arbh-206–214. If your teacher really did teach it your way, say
   so and I'll swap them back.
2. **qud-224 is narrowed, not reversed.** It used to say that for any pure liquid, at least two of water's
   three qualities must remain. Your new rule makes that true only for a liquid with all three qualities of
   its own. A liquid with only two overwhelms the water after one change, and that's a new card, qud-229.

## Decisions you should know about

- **"Certainty is resurrection"** reads as a slip for *certainty in* resurrection (arbh-200).
- **"Plain water: māʾ al-muṭlaq" under ṭahūr** is already covered: qud-215 says māʾ muṭlaq is always ṭahūr.
  No new card.
- **"Don't be complacent"** is already arbh-198. No new card.
- **"A whole study to these proofs"** is added to arbh-183 rather than made a card of its own.
- **The large-quantity card is split.** qud-228 now has the area (10 × 10, or 36 around), and the depth
  ("leaning down to scoop, you wouldn't see the bottom") is its own card, qud-232.
- **"kasb" is glossed "acquisition"**, its usual translation. Your notes don't translate it.
- **The cat:** your notes say it's an exception "because cats live among human beings". The card says it
  is still ṭahūr but disliked. Your notes don't say what it's an exception *to* (the leftovers of predatory
  animals are otherwise impure), so the card doesn't either.

- **All Tier 1.**
