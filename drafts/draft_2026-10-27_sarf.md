## Check this first

1. **The dropped tāʾ (sarf-249):** your note says yataqabbalu can become yaqabbalu. It's the forms
   that begin with tāʾ that drop one: tataqabbalu becomes taqabbalu, as in "tanazzalu l-malāʾikah" (97:4). The
   yāʾ forms keep both letters. The card says that.
2. **The merged tāʾ (sarf-250):** it's izzammala, with a doubled zāy. The hamzat al-waṣl is there
   because the word now starts with a sākin letter, not for iltiqāʾ al-sākinayn.
3. **The ṣarf ṣaghīr examples are mine.** Your notes name the abwāb without examples, so I used the traditional ones
   that match your class's ṣarrafa and taqabbala: akrama, taqabbala, taqābala, infaṭara, ijtanaba, istanṣara,
   baʿthara and tasarbala. Tell me if your teacher uses others.

## Decisions you should know about

- **Same design as tafʿīl and mufāʿalah on 10-23,** per your note: one two-way card per form, with the bāb's maṣdar
  name on it, and a ṣarf ṣaghīr table per bāb.
- **Infiʿāl has no majhūl,** as your notes say, so it has no majhūl cards and its table has no majhūl row. Its ẓarf
  gets its own blank. Why it has none (it's intransitive) is added from what I know.
- **Ifʿilāl (IX) is skipped,** as in class.
