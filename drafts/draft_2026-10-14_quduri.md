### Ikhtilāf — the strength of evidence (8)

| id | Type | Front | Back |
|---|---|---|---|
| qud-272 | basic | On what must the answer to any Islamic question be based? | Evidence — <bdi>الدَّلِيل</bdi>, which is usually textual |
| qud-273 | bidir | <bdi>قَطْعِيّ</bdi> (qaṭʿī) | definitive — of evidence, wholly certain |
| qud-274 | bidir | <bdi>ظَنِّيّ</bdi> (ẓannī) | speculative — of evidence, short of certain, though it can still be very strong |
| qud-275 | bidir | <bdi>الثُّبُوت</bdi> (al-thubūt) | a proof's transmission — how it reached us: by tawātur, or as āḥād |
| qud-276 | bidir | <bdi>الدَّلَالَة</bdi> (al-dalālah) | a proof's signification — how clear its meaning is, and whether it is open to interpretation |
| qud-277 | basic | What is the highest standard of evidence? | <bdi>قَطْعِيّ الثُّبُوت وَقَطْعِيّ الدَّلَالَة</bdi> — transmitted by tawātur, with a meaning not open to interpretation |
| qud-278 | cloze | Disagreement is allowed where a proof is ẓannī in some respect: <bdi>قَطْعِيّ الثُّبُوت، ظَنِّيّ الدَّلَالَة</bdi> — {{c1::its source is certain, but people differ on its meaning}}; <bdi>ظَنِّيّ الثُّبُوت، قَطْعِيّ الدَّلَالَة</bdi> — {{c2::its meaning is clear, but its transmission is weaker}}; <bdi>ظَنِّيّ الثُّبُوت، ظَنِّيّ الدَّلَالَة</bdi> — {{c3::both are speculative}} | Only the qaṭʿī–qaṭʿī proof leaves no room for disagreement. |
| qud-279 | basic | How certain is a ẓannī proof, compared with a qaṭʿī one? | A qaṭʿī proof is 100% certain. A ẓannī one is not, but its certainty can still be very high — ẓannī doesn't mean 0%. |
