# Draft — Arbaʿīn Ḥadīth 8 (16 notes) and its vocab list (8 notes)

Status: **awaiting approval.**

Source: the *Hadith #8* toggle on the Arbaʿīn/Qudūrī page, fetched 2026-10-28. Session tag `oct27`.

## Check this first

1. **The translation is mine.** The notes have none, so, as with Ḥadīth 4, 6 and 7, I wrote one. It's in
   `texts/arbaeen_h08.md` with the matn. One card rests on it alone: until when the fighting was commanded.
2. **"Every time Allah mentions iman it is mentioned with action"** is softened to "again and again". The Qurʾān
   does sometimes mention īmān alone (49:14, for one), so "every time" would teach something false.
3. **Your notes end "(to be continued)".** This is everything up to there. The rest gets carded at the next scan.

## Decisions you should know about

- **The vocab goes in the vocab deck,** tagged `h08` like the other Arbaʿīn lists: four verbs, each with a meaning
  card and a paradigm. قَاتَلَ (to fight) gets both maṣdars, as you asked for mufāʿalah.
- **Added from what I know:** 2:256 ("no compulsion in religion") as the Qurʾānic evidence on forcing people into
  Islam, the Arabic names of the five maqāṣid, and the example verse on īmān and action.
- **The five maqāṣid follow your order.** Scholars order them differently.
- **Ibn ʿUmar's biography isn't repeated.** It's carded under Ḥadīth 3; here there's only the narrator card.
- **All Tier 1.**
