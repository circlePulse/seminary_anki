## What changes

- **The sunnah muʾakkadah lists now run to fourteen,** on three cards: 1–5 (tah-027), 6–9 (qud-154) and 10–14
  (qud-167). The last card holds the five that al-Qudūrī lists as mustaḥabb: the intention, wiping the whole head,
  sequence, beginning with the right, and doing it consecutively.
- **Wiping the neck is the only mustaḥabb action** (qud-166, asked both ways).
- **Al-Qudūrī's own heading now appears on one card only** (qud-165), which says his list is an oversight. It's also
  in the small print on the back of qud-167.
- **The intention in wuḍūʾ** is now "a sunnah muʾakkadah" on the tayammum contrast card (qud-270), where it said
  "only recommended".
- **The old sixth blank of qud-167** was "wiping the neck". It now asks what category the five actions are, so no card
  goes blank in Anki.
