## Check this first

1. **الها** is read as **أَلْهَى**, to distract (أَلْهَاكُمُ التَّكَاثُرُ), with ا written for ى, as in your أدنا
   for أَدْنَى. If you meant something else, such as إِلٰهًا ("a god"), tell me.
2. **بوصل** is read as **يُوصِلُ**, "it leads to", with ب written for ي. The commentaries gloss يَهْدِي that way:
   الصِّدْقُ يَهْدِي إِلَى البِرِّ, truthfulness leads to righteousness.
3. **يقلق** is unvowelled. It's carded as قَلِقَ يَقْلَقُ, to be anxious. If yours was يُقْلِقُ ("it unsettles"),
   that's أَقْلَقَ, and the extra on the card says so.

## Decisions you should know about

- **Slips corrected:**
  - ه written for ة: الضراعه, العُده, مطابقه, طمأنينه.
  - ا written for ى: أدنا → أَدْنَى.
  - كُلفتٍ → كُلْفَة.
  - الإفتقار → الاِفْتِقَار, since it takes hamzat al-waṣl.
- **Maṣdars get their verb's paradigm**, as حَثّ did on 10-06. That covers اِفْتِقَار, نَيْل, تَحْرِيض, مُطَابَقَة,
  اِسْتِوَاء, فُجُور, طُمَأْنِينَة, كَوْن and اِرْتِيَاب. For طُمَأْنِينَة the paradigm gives the regular maṣdar,
  اِطْمِئْنَان, and the extra notes that طُمَأْنِينَة is its ism maṣdar.
- **Meaning card only**, with no plural or paradigm:
  - ضَرَاعَة: its verb's bāb varies between sources.
  - حَوْل: its plural أَحْوَال is mostly the plural of حَال.
  - الرَّاجِح, المَرْجُوح and الوَاقِع: they're terms, and their plurals aren't used.
- **أَدْنَى gets a masculine/feminine card instead of a plural:** أَدْنَى — دُنْيَا. That's the أَفْعَلُ / فُعْلَى
  pattern from Thursday's ṣarf, and it's where الدُّنْيَا comes from.
- **Two senses kept apart:**
  - حَوْل is power, and also a full year in zakāh.
  - عُدَّة (equipment) is not عِدَّة (a woman's waiting period); the plural card notes this.
- **Gloss index:** none of the 26 glosses collides with an existing card.
- **Where these seem to come from:** يَرِيبُ, طُمَأْنِينَة, اِرْتِيَاب and الفُجُور point to the ḥadīth on truthfulness
  (دَعْ مَا يَرِيبُكَ…, إِنَّ الصِّدْقَ يَهْدِي إِلَى البِرِّ…). Tell me the text and I'll tag the list.
