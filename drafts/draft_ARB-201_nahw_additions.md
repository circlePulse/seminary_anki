## Questions

I carded it as "exclamation — calling out to someone" to keep your wording while making the front unambiguous. Confirm, or I'll switch it to plain "the vocative".

## One existing card was corrected

`nahw-061` said the two types of جُمْلَة تَامَّة are فِعْلِيَّة and اِسْمِيَّة. The new material makes that wrong: the جُمْلَة divides into خَبَرِيَّة and إِنْشَائِيَّة first, and اِسْمِيَّة/فِعْلِيَّة sit *under* خَبَرِيَّة. The card now asks for the top-level division; a new card covers the subdivision. This is the spine-editing case — the tree grew and an old card silently became false.

## Decisions

- **مَاضِي not re-carded.** Already a bidirectional note in the Ṣarf deck with the same sense. Logged rather than duplicated.
- **الأَمْر and النَّهْي appear twice** — as types of فِعْل and as types of جُمْلَة إِنْشَائِيَّة. One bidirectional note each plus a placement card naming both classifications, rather than two identical fronts.
- **نَاقِص/غَيْر مُفِيد and تَامّ/مُفِيد** carried by one synonym cloze. The existing definition cards and the مُرَكَّب spine were left alone — same reasoning as كَلِمَة/مُفْرَد: synonymy is carded once, never as two bidirs sharing a gloss.
- **مُسْنَد / مُسْنَد إِلَيْهِ get a dedicated contrast note.** They swap position between nominal and verbal sentences, which is the single most confusable thing in this batch, plus a card on what stays fixed.
- **Ten types of إِنْشَائِيَّة**: one enumeration card with a grouping hint, plus a bidirectional note per type keyed on its own content — no position-based cards.
- **التَّمَنِّي vs التَّرَجِّي** get a criterion contrast — near-synonyms in English, and your notes supply the distinguishing test.
