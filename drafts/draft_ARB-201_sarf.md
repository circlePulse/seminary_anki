## Questions

**Blocking — one:**

1. **مُفْرَد is now overloaded across two decks.** In the Naḥw deck it means *a single word* (= كَلِمَة). In Ṣarf it means *singular* (number). Same Arabic word, two glosses, both already in your collection — which is the exact collision the gloss index exists to catch, and the exact thing your own §1.1 note warns about ("terms overlap between the two sciences"). Two fixes needed:
   - In this deck, مُفْرَد is always qualified as a number term.
   - **Naḥw card 23** ("What is a مُفْرَد?" → "A single word") should become "What is a مُفْرَد **in نَحْو**?". One edit in the browser, or I regenerate the Naḥw deck.

**Calls I've made — override if wrong, otherwise no need to reply:**

2. **Charts carded as text, one cloze per row** (revised per your instruction). Each row keeps the book's own layout, with the Arabic and the grammatical description as separate deletions — so one note gives both production and recognition without two competing fronts.
3. **المُخَاطَبَانِ / المُخَاطَبَتَانِ left as the book has them** — both rows stand as their own cards. The meta-card pointing out that their مَاضِي is identical is demoted to Tier 2 rather than cut: it ships suspended, costs nothing, and is there if a future exam asks.
4. **Definition of صَرْف carded here** despite being covered in §1.1, per your instruction.
5. **The four-تاء forms are one contrast note, not four sibling cards.** Four fronts distinguished only by tashkīl is a guaranteed leech cluster. As a cloze, each deletion shows the other three, so the disambiguation is visible at review time.
6. **فَتَحَ principal parts** (يَفْتَحُ / اِفْتَحْ / فَتْح) added as Tier 2 — the notes give only the māḍī, but it's the model verb for the whole section.
7. **Suffix-pattern generalisation** (ـَة for fem. sing., ـَانِ for dual, etc.) added as Tier 2 — readable off the chart, but not stated in the lecture.
8. **Not added:** the literal meanings of غَائِب / مُخَاطَب / مُتَكَلِّم ("the absent one", "the one addressed", "the one speaking"). They make the whole system click, but they weren't taught. Say the word and I'll add three cards.
