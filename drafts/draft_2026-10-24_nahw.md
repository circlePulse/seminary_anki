## Check this first

1. **Nūn al-wiqāyah:** your notes say it comes "when the ending requires a sukoon but there isn't one naturally".
   It's a kasrah, not a sukūn. The speaker's yāʾ needs a kasrah before it, and a verb can't take one, so a nūn is
   added to carry it: ḍarabanī (ضَرَبَنِي), not ḍarabī. The cards (nahw-229) say that. Tell me if your teacher
   meant something else.
2. **The relative-pronoun chart is mine** (nahw-235). Your note asks to memorise the chart, but the chart isn't
   on the page. I built the standard one. Check it against your class's, especially the feminine plural: I gave
   allātī (اللَّاتِي) and allawātī (اللَّوَاتِي), and some charts add allāʾī (اللَّائِي).
