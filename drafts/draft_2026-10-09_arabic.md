## Decisions you should know about

- **Two cards for the shared patterns, not in the notes.** Your notes list فَعِيل and فَعُول under both
  الصِّفَة المُشَبَّهَة and اِسْم المُبَالَغَة, and مِفْعَال under both اِسْم المُبَالَغَة and اِسْم الآلَة. So one
  card asks what tells the first pair apart, using your own definitions (a long-term quality vs more
  intensity). The other asks which two nouns share مِفْعَال. The examples (كَرِيم, غَفُور, مِقْدَام, مِفْتَاح)
  are mine. Neither is an either/or card, which you could answer by elimination.
- **"To avoid duplications"**, for the dual keeping its alif, is read as "so it isn't confused with the
  singular" (يَفْتَحَانِّ vs يَفْتَحَنَّ).
- **The two Qurʾān places for the light nūn** are named: لَنَسْفَعًا (96:15) and وَلَيَكُونًا (12:32).
- **Other examples added:** عَالِم / عَلَّام / عَلِيم, أَكْبَرُ, أَكْثَرُ اسْتِغْفَارًا, مَجْلِس / مَكْتَب / مَدْرَسَة, and
  مِبْرَد / مِكْنَسَة / مِفْتَاح.
- **"A tool to do an action"** is already sarf-190. No new card.
- **Your rule of thumb (guess ẓarf before ālah)** would make a two-option question, and those are useless for
  testing. It's on the back of the ālah-patterns card instead, where it matters: reading an unvowelled
  word.
- **Slips corrected:** "Seegha muballaqhah" → صِيغَة المُبَالَغَة; "fa’’’aal" → فَعَّال.
