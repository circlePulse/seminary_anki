## Check this first

1. **"Less than a verse is ok"** (qud-318) is al-Ṭaḥāwī's view. Al-Hidāyah and the later Ḥanafīs forbid even
   less than a verse when it's meant as recitation, and that's the school's relied-upon position. The card gives both.
   Tell me if your teacher meant otherwise.
2. **Your note "Carries the ruling of ____" is blank.** Qudūrī says istiḥāḍah is like a constant nosebleed, so the card
   (qud-324) fills that in.
3. **The gap of purity** (qud-311) is a gap within the days of ḥayḍ, as your example says, not between
   two cycles.

## Decisions you should know about

- **Corrected without asking:** "3 days and lights" is nights, istiHaarah is istiḥāḍah, and ma'thurah is maʿdhūrah.
- **Added from Qudūrī's text:** "until she sees pure white", on the colour card.
- **The exception for a teacher or student** doesn't extend to major ritual impurity, as your notes say. That's on its
  card, since a yes-or-no card of its own isn't allowed.
- **All Tier 1.**
