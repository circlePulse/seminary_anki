## Notes on decomposition

- **The criterion was recoverable.** "Any sound humans make is lafDH. If it has meaning, it is called mawDoo'un or muhmalun" is incoherent as written — one conditional can't govern both branches. But the child bullet ("waDa'a … is to enter something into the dictionary") supplies exactly the missing criterion, so this was a mechanical gap, filled rather than flagged: the division is by whether a meaning has been **assigned** (وَضْع).
- **مُفْرَد/كَلِمَة and كَلَام/جُمْلَة are synonym pairs.** Giving each member its own bidirectional note would put the same English gloss on two fronts. Instead the synonymy is carded once and the pair appears together in the spine note.
- **Transliteration normalised** to academic convention (lafDH → لَفْظ / lafẓ). Arabic supplied with tashkīl throughout; the notes are transliteration-only.
