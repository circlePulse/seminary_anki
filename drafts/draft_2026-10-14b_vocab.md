# Draft — Ḥadīth 6 verbs, 2026-10-14 (14 new notes, 2 retagged)

Status: **approved 2026-10-14 and delivered.**

Source: eight māḍī/muḍāriʿ pairs pasted into the chat in transliteration on 2026-10-14. They are the verbs of
Ḥadīth 6 (al-ḥalālu bayyin), in the order they occur in it, so they're tagged `arbaeen` and `h06`, like the
Ḥadīth 2 and 3 verbs.

## Decisions you should know about

- **One correction:** your yar'ee is يَرْعَى (yarʿā), as in كَالرَّاعِي يَرْعَى حَوْلَ الحِمَى. The ع in its middle
  is a throat letter, so the muḍāriʿ takes fatḥah, and the ى stays. (Your ra'aaa is read as رَعَى.)
- **The opening hamza:** your initial ' is right for أَوْشَكَ, whose Form IV alif is hamzat al-qaṭʿ. In
  اِشْتَبَهَ (Form VIII), اِتَّقَى (Form VIII) and اِسْتَبْرَأَ (Form X) it's hamzat al-waṣl, which the ḥadīth
  drops in فَمَنِ اتَّقَى and in فَقَدِ اسْتَبْرَأَ.
- **Already in the deck:** صَلَحَ يَصْلُحُ is voc-176/177, so it isn't carded twice. Both notes gain the `arbaeen`
  and `h06` tags, so the ḥadīth's verbs filter together. The card for فَسَدَ names صَلَحَ as its opposite.
- **The verb أَوْشَكَ has a one-blank cloze**, for its muḍāriʿ only. Its amr and maṣdar aren't in use. The extra
  notes that it's a verb of nearness whose khabar takes أَنْ, as in يُوشِكُ أَنْ يَرْتَعَ فِيهِ.
- **Two grazing verbs, kept apart:** the gloss for رَعَى is "to graze (livestock)", the shepherd's work. The gloss
  for رَتَعَ is "to roam and graze at will", the flock's freedom.
- **Gloss index:** none of the 7 new meaning cards collides with an existing card.
- **Examples** come from the ḥadīth itself, and from Yūsuf 12:12 for رَتَعَ.
- **Session tag `oct14`. All Tier 1.**
