## Check this first

1. **Al-Nuʿmān ibn Bashīr's death:** your notes say he was killed "in the year 56". The sources put it at the end
   of 64 AH, some say 65, at Ḥimṣ. Your 56 looks like 65 with the digits swapped, so the card says "64 or 65 AH".
2. **The translation is mine.** The notes have none, so, as with Ḥadīth 4, I wrote one. It's in
   `texts/arbaeen_h06.md` with the matn. One card rests on it: Allah's sanctuary is what He has made unlawful.

## Decisions you should know about

- **Already carded, so not repeated:**
  - Imam Aḥmad's three foundational ḥadīth are arbh-232, from Ḥadīth 5.
  - The ḥadīth's verbs are voc-324 to voc-337, from 10-14. Your vocab list now reads yar'aa, matching the card,
    and its wa'aa is a slip for ra'aa.
- **One new vocab card:** أَلَا, the particle that calls attention, which the ḥadīth repeats four times.
- **Merged:** the translation's lines on guarding against doubtful matters, and on falling into them, are folded
  into the cards built from your notes on the same points.
- **The ḥimā** is named on the shepherd card, as the private pasture your notes call "someone else's property".
- **Qudūrī:**
  - Abū Yūsuf's view on materials gets its own card.
  - qud-269, the materials card, now says the rule excludes plants and wood, and that it's Abū Ḥanīfah's and
    Muḥammad's view.
  - Your notes fixed "thirts" themselves, and the card already said thirst.
