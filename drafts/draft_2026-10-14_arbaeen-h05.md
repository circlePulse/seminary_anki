# Draft — Arbaʿīn Ḥadīth 5, on innovation (24 + 2 new notes)

Status: **approved 2026-10-14 and delivered.**

Source: the new *Hadith #5* toggle on the Arbaʿīn/Qudūrī page, as fetched on 2026-10-14. Session tag `oct14`.
Ḥadīth 6 is untouched, as its CLAUDE note asks.

## Check this first

1. **When ʿĀʾishah RA moved in:** your notes say the second year after the Hijrah, and the card follows them.
   Most sources put it in Shawwāl of year 1, and some put it in year 2, so check what your teacher said.
2. **Her death:** your notes say she lived "40 years" after the Prophet ﷺ and died in 57 AH. That's 46 years,
   since he died in 11 AH. The card says "more than forty years", which fits both.

## Corrections and additions

- **A slip corrected:** your notes say a deed that isn't sincere "will be accepted". It's *not* accepted, which is
  the point of the pairing with the ḥadīth on intentions (arbh-233).
- **Tarāwīḥ:** ʿUmar RA didn't originate tarāwīḥ. The Prophet ﷺ led it on some nights, and ʿUmar gathered the
  people behind one imām. The card says that, with ʿUmar's words.
- **From the commentaries (Nawawī), not your notes:**
  - Why Muslim's second wording is wider: it covers anyone who does the act, not only the one who began it.
  - The name جَوَامِع الكَلِم, for "concise and comprehensive sayings".
  - The nephew's name, ʿAbd Allāh ibn al-Zubayr.
  - The verse reference al-Anʿām 6:153.

## Decisions you should know about

- **Your CLAUDE note** asked for the vocab to be treated like a pasted list. أَحْدَثَ goes to the vocab deck as a
  verb and its paradigm (voc-338, voc-339), tagged `h05`. Its card adds the fiqh sense, to break one's wuḍūʾ,
  which you'll meet in Qudūrī. "The Prophet ﷺ used أَحْدَثَ, which literally means to invent" is carried by that
  vocab card rather than repeated here.
- **Imam Aḥmad's three ḥadīth** weren't carded when your notes first covered them, so they're carded now.
- **The matn** is saved in `texts/arbaeen_h05.md` for reference, as Ḥadīth 4's was. It's not carded.
- **arbh-240 became a cloze after approval:** as a question ("which way does the change run?") it was a
  two-way choice.
- **All Tier 1.**
