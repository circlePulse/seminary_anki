## Decisions you should know about

- **Inflected forms are carded under their verb.** يَنْقَضِيَ is under اِنْقَضَى, يَتَصَوَّرُ under تَصَوَّرَ, يُطَاقُ under أَطَاقَ, and يحتمل under اِحْتَمَلَ. Each paradigm's extra says what your form is.
- **إختار has a slip:** the alif is hamzat al-waṣl (اِخْتَارَ), so no hamza goes under it.
- **يحتمل** is unvowelled. It can be active (يَحْتَمِلُ, "it admits of") or passive (يُحْتَمَلُ, "it is possible"), and the extra gives both. The gloss leads with the interpretive sense, "to admit of (a meaning)", because المُرَاد is on the same list. Its other sense, "to put up with", is kept off "to bear", which is already تَحَمَّلَ (voc-064).
- **فَاتَ and اِنْقَضَى are kept off "to pass"**, which is already مَضَى (voc-084).
- **أَطَاقَ is glossed "to be capable of"**, not "to bear", for the same reason.
- **Every noun gets a singular/plural cloze**, as in the earlier lists, including regular plurals (رَاسِخُونَ, مَآلَات, تَسْلِيَات).
- **حَثّ has no plural in use**, so it gets its verb's paradigm instead (حَثَّ — يَحُثُّ — حُثَّ — حَثّ).
- **المُرَاد** gets a meaning card only, because its plural is rare. **لَا جَرَمَ, مَهْمَا and حَسَبَ** get meaning cards only, as particles did before.
- **The words are carded without ال** (رُخْصَة, قِسْمَة, مَشَقَّة, حَثّ, مَآل). The exception is المُرَاد, which is used that way.
- **Gloss index:** none of the 21 glosses collides with an existing card.
