## Check this first

1. **Zhu Xi on human nature:** your notes say he didn't take up whether human nature is good. He did. He sided with
   Mengzi, holding that human nature is principle (li) and so good, and that evil comes from the material force
   (qi) that clouds it. The card (wrel-202) gives his actual view. Drop it if you'd rather
   not have a card that disagrees with the lecture.
2. **The Enigmatic Female:** your notes call her "a thread between Heaven and earth". Chapter 6 calls her gate the
   root of Heaven and earth, so the card (wrel-223) says that. If your translation really says
   thread, tell me.
3. **Daodejing 42:** your notes go from the two, yin and yang, straight to everything. The text has a step between:
   the two produce the three, and the three produce everything. The card (wrel-248)
   includes it.
4. **A sentence that breaks off:** your notes say Daoism distrusts words and society, "both of which can lead
   people". I finished it as "lead people astray" (wrel-266).
