## Decisions you should know about

- **Tagged `riyad-tawakkul`,** for Bāb al-Yaqīn wa-l-Tawakkul.
- **Already in the deck:** your يُنال is the passive of nāla (نَالَ يَنَالُ), which is voc-243 and voc-244. It isn't
  carded again, but voc-244's extra now gives the passive, “it is attained”.
- **Adjectives and participles get a meaning card only:** maḥẓūr (forbidden) and munīf (lofty).
- **Plurals:** badan (body) and khirqah (rag) each get a singular–plural card.
- **The maṣdar tasdīd** gets its verb's paradigm, from saddada.
- **The examples in the extras are mine.** They're the ḥadīth “saddidū wa-qāribū”, the ḥadīth on the one who strives
  in Allah's path, and “ṭāfa bi-l-bayt”.
- **Gloss index:** none of the new meanings collides with an existing one.
- **All Tier 1.**
