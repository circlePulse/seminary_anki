## Decisions you should know about

- **Examples taken from the matn, not your notes.** Three things come from al-Qudūrī's own text:
  - the fly and scorpion (no flowing blood) and the fish and frog (living in water);
  - the dog, pig and beasts of prey as the animals whose suʾr is impure;
  - the attribution of the one-day and three-day repetition to **Abū Ḥanīfah**. Your notes imply it by
    setting Abū Yūsuf and Muḥammad against it, and the matn states it.
- **Your "and vice versa"** (an animal not ḥalāl to eat leaves an impure suʾr) is carded as the general
  rule. The spine card (qud-254) gives the exceptions your notes list right after it: house animals such
  as the cat are only disliked, and the donkey and mule are doubtful.
- **The cat appears twice on purpose.** qud-234/235 (from the water section) cover water a cat drank from.
  The suʾr cards cover the general category, animals that live in houses. They agree.
- **Arabic terms added**, all from the matn: مَيْتَة, دِبَاغ, نَزْح, السُّؤْر, and the four rulings.
- **Slip corrected:** "(human, sheep dog)" → a human, a sheep, a dog.
- **Ḥadīth 4:** the saying is carded with its Arabic, إِنَّمَا الأَعْمَالُ بِالخَوَاتِيمِ (al-Bukhārī). That
  makes one small Arbaʿīn file alongside the Qudūrī one.

| qud-250 | basic | Besides repeating prayers, what must be done when a well whose water was used turns out to hold a dead animal? | Everything its water touched must be purified |

| qud-254 | cloze | Rulings on <bdi>سُؤْر</bdi>: {{c1::pure — <bdi>طَاهِر</bdi>}} for humans and animals ḥalāl to eat; {{c2::impure — <bdi>نَجِس</bdi>}} for animals that are not, such as the dog, the pig and beasts of prey; {{c3::disliked — <bdi>مَكْرُوه</bdi>}} for animals that commonly live in houses, such as the cat; {{c4::doubtful — <bdi>مَشْكُوك فِيهِ</bdi>}} for the donkey and the mule |  |
