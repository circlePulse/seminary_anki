## Decisions you should know about

- **Your checked list is included,** with ḥadīth 7, 16 and 25 and the phrase in 29. It's required memorisation too, and
  the cards keep it fresh. Say if you'd rather leave them out.
- **Each ḥadīth has two kinds of card:**
  - **A cloze of the whole text,** one blank per phrase, from 2 to 9 cards per ḥadīth. Each blank is shown in bold
    below, numbered by its card.
  - **A recitation card:** the number, chapter and narrator on the front, and the whole text on the back. This is the
    real test, since the clozes always show the rest of the text.
- **Mirror-image phrases share a blank,** so neither half gives the other away. That covers night and day in 16, took
  and gave in 29, the truth and lie halves of 54, and missed and befell in 62.
- **The backs carry my translation** and the source, such as Bukhārī and Muslim.
- **The header on each card** gives the number and chapter, small and grey above the text.
- **The derived-noun chart is the ism al-ālah chart** (sarf-247). The screenshot opened at last. It gives the dual and
  plural of each pattern. There are two cards: the three duals, and then the three plurals. The two plurals that are the
  same would give each other away as separate blanks. The singular patterns stay visible; they're already sarf-204.
- **All Tier 1.**

## Cards

### Ḥadīth 7 — riyad-011 (cloze, 2 cards) and riyad-012 (recitation)

Header: Riyāḍ al-Ṣāliḥīn 7 · <bdi>بَابُ الإِخْلَاصِ</bdi>

<div dir="rtl">

إِنَّ اللهَ **لَا يَنْظُرُ إِلَى أَجْسَامِكُمْ، وَلَا إِلَى صُوَرِكُمْ**<sup>1</sup>، **وَلَكِنْ يَنْظُرُ إِلَى قُلُوبِكُمْ وَأَعْمَالِكُمْ**<sup>2</sup>

</div>

- **Recitation front:** Recite Abū Hurayrah's ḥadīth on what Allah looks at.
- **Extra on the cloze:** Abū Hurayrah, from the Prophet ﷺ: “Allah does not look at your bodies or your forms, but He looks at your hearts and your deeds.” (Muslim)

### Ḥadīth 16 — riyad-013 (cloze, 2 cards) and riyad-014 (recitation)

Header: Riyāḍ al-Ṣāliḥīn 16 · <bdi>بَابُ التَّوْبَةِ</bdi>

<div dir="rtl">

إِنَّ اللهَ تَعَالَى يَبْسُطُ يَدَهُ **بِاللَّيْلِ لِيَتُوبَ مُسِيءُ النَّهَارِ**<sup>1</sup>، وَيَبْسُطُ يَدَهُ **بِالنَّهَارِ لِيَتُوبَ مُسِيءُ اللَّيْلِ**<sup>1</sup>، **حَتَّى تَطْلُعَ الشَّمْسُ مِنْ مَغْرِبِهَا**<sup>2</sup>

</div>

- **Recitation front:** Recite Abū Mūsā al-Ashʿarī's ḥadīth on Allah stretching out His hand to those who repent.
- **Extra on the cloze:** Abū Mūsā al-Ashʿarī, from the Prophet ﷺ: “Allah the Exalted stretches out His hand by night so that the one who did wrong by day may repent, and stretches out His hand by day so that the one who did wrong by night may repent — until the sun rises from the west.” (Muslim)

### Ḥadīth 25 — riyad-015 (cloze, 9 cards) and riyad-016 (recitation)

Header: Riyāḍ al-Ṣāliḥīn 25 · <bdi>بَابُ الصَّبْرِ</bdi>

<div dir="rtl">

**الطُّهُورُ شَطْرُ الإِيمَانِ**<sup>1</sup>، **وَالحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ تَمْلَأُ المِيزَانَ**<sup>2</sup>، **وَسُبْحَانَ اللهِ وَالحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ تَمْلَآنِ -أَوْ تَمْلَأُ- مَا بَيْنَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالأَرْضِ**<sup>3</sup>، **وَالصَّلَاةُ نُورٌ**<sup>4</sup>، **وَالصَّدَقَةُ بُرْهَانٌ**<sup>5</sup>، **وَالصَّبْرُ ضِيَاءٌ**<sup>6</sup>، **وَالقُرْآنُ حُجَّةٌ لَكَ أَوْ عَلَيْكَ**<sup>7</sup>، **كُلُّ النَّاسِ يَغْدُو**<sup>8</sup>، **فَبَائِعٌ نَفْسَهُ فَمُعْتِقُهَا أَوْ مُوبِقُهَا**<sup>9</sup>

</div>

- **Recitation front:** Recite Abū Mālik al-Ashʿarī's ḥadīth, the first in the chapter on patience.
- **Extra on the cloze:** Abū Mālik al-Ashʿarī, from the Prophet ﷺ: “Purity is half of faith. Al-ḥamdu lillāh fills the scale, and subḥān Allāh and al-ḥamdu lillāh fill — or: fills — what is between the heavens and the earth. Prayer is light, charity is proof, patience is radiance, and the Qurʾān is an argument for you or against you. Everyone sets out in the morning selling himself, and so frees himself or ruins himself.” (Muslim)

### Ḥadīth 29, the phrase to his daughter — riyad-017 (cloze, 3 cards) and riyad-018 (recitation)

Header: Riyāḍ al-Ṣāliḥīn 29 · <bdi>بَابُ الصَّبْرِ</bdi>

<div dir="rtl">

**إِنَّ لِلَّهِ مَا أَخَذَ، وَلَهُ مَا أَعْطَى**<sup>1</sup>، **وَكُلُّ شَيْءٍ عِنْدَهُ بِأَجَلٍ مُسَمًّى**<sup>2</sup>، **فَلْتَصْبِرْ وَلْتَحْتَسِبْ**<sup>3</sup>

</div>

- **Recitation front:** Recite what the Prophet ﷺ sent to tell his daughter as her son was dying (from Usāmah ibn Zayd).
- **Extra on the cloze:** Usāmah ibn Zayd reports that the Prophet's ﷺ daughter sent for him as her son was dying, and he sent back his salām and these words: “To Allah belongs what He took, and to Him belongs what He gave, and everything has an appointed term with Him; so let her be patient and hope for the reward.” (Bukhārī and Muslim)

### Ḥadīth 37 — riyad-019 (cloze, 6 cards) and riyad-020 (recitation)

Header: Riyāḍ al-Ṣāliḥīn 37 · <bdi>بَابُ الصَّبْرِ</bdi>

<div dir="rtl">

**مَا يُصِيبُ المُسْلِمَ**<sup>1</sup> مِنْ **نَصَبٍ، وَلَا وَصَبٍ**<sup>2</sup>، **وَلَا هَمٍّ، وَلَا حُزْنٍ**<sup>3</sup>، **وَلَا أَذًى، وَلَا غَمٍّ**<sup>4</sup>، **حَتَّى الشَّوْكَةِ يُشَاكُهَا**<sup>5</sup>، **إِلَّا كَفَّرَ اللهُ بِهَا مِنْ خَطَايَاهُ**<sup>6</sup>

</div>

- **Recitation front:** Recite the ḥadīth of Abū Saʿīd and Abū Hurayrah on the hardships that befall a Muslim.
- **Extra on the cloze:** Abū Saʿīd and Abū Hurayrah, from the Prophet ﷺ: “No fatigue, illness, worry, grief, harm or distress befalls a Muslim — not even a thorn that pricks him — but that Allah expiates some of his sins by it.” (Bukhārī and Muslim)<br>Al-Nawawī glosses <bdi>الوَصَب</bdi> as illness. <bdi>حَتَّى الشَّوْكَةِ</bdi> is also read <bdi>حَتَّى الشَّوْكَةُ</bdi>.

### Ḥadīth 54 — riyad-021 (cloze, 2 cards) and riyad-022 (recitation)

Header: Riyāḍ al-Ṣāliḥīn 54 · <bdi>بَابُ الصِّدْقِ</bdi>

<div dir="rtl">

**إِنَّ الصِّدْقَ يَهْدِي إِلَى البِرِّ، وَإِنَّ البِرَّ يَهْدِي إِلَى الجَنَّةِ**<sup>1</sup>، **وَإِنَّ الرَّجُلَ لَيَصْدُقُ حَتَّى يُكْتَبَ عِنْدَ اللهِ صِدِّيقًا**<sup>2</sup>، **وَإِنَّ الكَذِبَ يَهْدِي إِلَى الفُجُورِ، وَإِنَّ الفُجُورَ يَهْدِي إِلَى النَّارِ**<sup>1</sup>، **وَإِنَّ الرَّجُلَ لَيَكْذِبُ حَتَّى يُكْتَبَ عِنْدَ اللهِ كَذَّابًا**<sup>2</sup>

</div>

- **Recitation front:** Recite Ibn Masʿūd's ḥadīth on where truthfulness and lying lead.
- **Extra on the cloze:** Ibn Masʿūd, from the Prophet ﷺ: “Truthfulness leads to righteousness, and righteousness leads to Paradise; a man keeps telling the truth until he is written with Allah as a man of truth. Lying leads to wickedness, and wickedness leads to the Fire; a man keeps lying until he is written with Allah as a liar.” (Bukhārī and Muslim)

### Ḥadīth 61 — riyad-023 (cloze, 3 cards) and riyad-024 (recitation)

Header: Riyāḍ al-Ṣāliḥīn 61 · <bdi>بَابُ المُرَاقَبَةِ</bdi>

<div dir="rtl">

**اتَّقِ اللهَ حَيْثُمَا كُنْتَ**<sup>1</sup>، **وَأَتْبِعِ السَّيِّئَةَ الحَسَنَةَ تَمْحُهَا**<sup>2</sup>، **وَخَالِقِ النَّاسَ بِخُلُقٍ حَسَنٍ**<sup>3</sup>

</div>

- **Recitation front:** Recite the three commands in the ḥadīth of Abū Dharr and Muʿādh ibn Jabal.
- **Extra on the cloze:** Abū Dharr and Muʿādh ibn Jabal, from the Prophet ﷺ: “Be mindful of Allah wherever you are; follow a bad deed with a good one and it will wipe it out; and treat people with good character.” (al-Tirmidhī, who graded it ḥasan)

### Ḥadīth 62, second narration — riyad-025 (cloze, 6 cards) and riyad-026 (recitation)

Header: Riyāḍ al-Ṣāliḥīn 62, second narration · <bdi>بَابُ المُرَاقَبَةِ</bdi>

<div dir="rtl">

**احْفَظِ اللهَ تَجِدْهُ أَمَامَكَ**<sup>1</sup>، **تَعَرَّفْ إِلَى اللهِ فِي الرَّخَاءِ يَعْرِفْكَ فِي الشِّدَّةِ**<sup>2</sup>، وَاعْلَمْ أَنَّ **مَا أَخْطَأَكَ لَمْ يَكُنْ لِيُصِيبَكَ، وَمَا أَصَابَكَ لَمْ يَكُنْ لِيُخْطِئَكَ**<sup>3</sup>، وَاعْلَمْ أَنَّ **النَّصْرَ مَعَ الصَّبْرِ**<sup>4</sup>، وَأَنَّ **الفَرَجَ مَعَ الكَرْبِ**<sup>5</sup>، وَأَنَّ **مَعَ العُسْرِ يُسْرًا**<sup>6</sup>

</div>

- **Recitation front:** Recite the second narration of Ibn ʿAbbās's ḥadīth <bdi>يَا غُلَامُ</bdi> (“O boy”), the one other than al-Tirmidhī's.
- **Extra on the cloze:** Ibn ʿAbbās, from the Prophet ﷺ: “Be mindful of Allah and you will find Him before you. Make yourself known to Allah in ease, and He will know you in hardship. Know that what missed you was never going to befall you, and what befell you was never going to miss you. And know that victory comes with patience, relief with distress, and with hardship comes ease.” (a narration other than al-Tirmidhī's)<br>Al-Tirmidhī's narration, which is Arbaʿīn 19, has <bdi>تُجَاهَكَ</bdi> where this one has <bdi>أَمَامَكَ</bdi>.

### Ḥadīth 82, the duʿāʾ — riyad-029 (cloze, 5 cards) and riyad-030 (recitation)

Header: Riyāḍ al-Ṣāliḥīn 82, the duʿāʾ · <bdi>بَابُ اليَقِينِ وَالتَّوَكُّلِ</bdi>

<div dir="rtl">

بِسْمِ اللهِ، **تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى اللهِ**<sup>1</sup>، اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ أَنْ **أَضِلَّ أَوْ أُضَلَّ**<sup>2</sup>، أَوْ **أَزِلَّ أَوْ أُزَلَّ**<sup>3</sup>، أَوْ **أَظْلِمَ أَوْ أُظْلَمَ**<sup>4</sup>، أَوْ **أَجْهَلَ أَوْ يُجْهَلَ عَلَيَّ**<sup>5</sup>

### The ism al-ālah chart — sarf-247 (cloze, 2 cards)

Card 1 blanks the three duals, and card 2 the three plurals:

<div dir="rtl">

| مُفْرَدٌ | مُثَنًّى | جَمْعٌ |
|---|---|---|
| مِفْعَلٌ | **مِفْعَلَانِ**<sup>1</sup> | **مَفَاعِلُ**<sup>2</sup> |
| مِفْعَلَةٌ | **مِفْعَلَتَانِ**<sup>1</sup> | **مَفَاعِلُ**<sup>2</sup> |
| مِفْعَالٌ | **مِفْعَالَانِ**<sup>1</sup> | **مَفَاعِيلُ**<sup>2</sup> |

</div>

- **Extra:** The chart of <bdi>اِسْم الآلَة</bdi>. <bdi>مِفْعَل</bdi> and <bdi>مِفْعَلَة</bdi> share the plural <bdi>مَفَاعِل</bdi>, and the long ā of <bdi>مِفْعَال</bdi> becomes the long ī of <bdi>مَفَاعِيل</bdi>: <bdi>مِبْرَد</bdi> (a file) makes <bdi>مَبَارِد</bdi>, <bdi>مِكْنَسَة</bdi> (a broom) makes <bdi>مَكَانِس</bdi>, and <bdi>مِفْتَاح</bdi> (a key) makes <bdi>مَفَاتِيح</bdi>.
