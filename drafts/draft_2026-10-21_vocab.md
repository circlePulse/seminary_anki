## Decisions you should know about

- **Where the Riyāḍ words come from:** Bāb al-Murāqabah, chapter 5, going by the three men of Banū Isrāʾīl
  (denial and acknowledgement of a blessing), "the astute one holds himself to account", and "leaving what
  doesn't concern him". They're tagged `riyad-muraqabah`, and the Qaṣaṣ words `qasas`. The older combined list
  kept its `riyad-qasas` tag.
- **Already in the deck, so not carded twice:**
  - The word الحث is voc-221 and voc-222.
  - The word مآربهم is voc-241 and voc-242.
  - The word كفّ is voc-294 and voc-295, from last week.
- **The verb صَارَ gets its paradigm only**, because its meaning card is nahw-197, as a sister of كَانَ.
- **Two slips corrected:**
  - Your الإعتراف takes hamzat al-waṣl: الاِعْتِرَاف.
  - Your شاطيء is spelt شَاطِئ, with the hamza on a ي seat.
- **One card for a pair:** your العاقبة and العواقب are the singular and plural of one noun.
- **Adjectives get a meaning card only:** عَاجِز (incapable), مُتَبَصِّر (discerning), مُطِيع (obedient) and
  مُنْقَاد (docile). Your مطيعة and منقادة are feminine, describing النَّفْس; the cards give the masculine.
- **Collectives:** خَشَبَة (a plank) and مَوْجَة (a wave) are carded as the single item. Their collectives, خَشَب and
  مَوْج, appear in the extras.
- **Gloss index:** none of the new meaning cards collides with an existing one. The word تَنَزَّهَ opens with "to
  stroll" rather than "to go out", which is خَرَجَ (voc-156).
- **All Tier 1.**
