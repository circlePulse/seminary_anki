## Check this first

1. **The Eightfold Path (wrel-299):** your notes list "right thought" and "right intent" as two
   steps and leave out "right mindfulness". Right thought and right intention are one step, translated two ways,
   and the missing one is right mindfulness. The card has the standard eight, in order. Your notes also group them
   as observation, action and mind, which isn't the usual grouping, so the card leaves the groups out. Tell me if
   your professor's grouping is one you'll be tested on.
2. **"Heaven = Sutras":** sutras are scriptures, so I couldn't tell what this means and didn't card it. If the
   lecture named a heaven, such as the Pure Land, tell me.
3. **Nirvana as "self-annihilation":** the word literally means "blowing out", of the fires of craving, and
   Buddhism doesn't describe it as the self being destroyed. The card (wrel-297) uses your other note
   instead: getting out of the cycle of reincarnation.
