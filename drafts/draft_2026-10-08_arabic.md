## Check this first

1. **Kāna and her sisters was missed on 2026-10-02.** It was already complete in your notes then, with its
   own Claude note ("memorize these … not overwhelming or a leech"). That batch carded the ḥarf sections
   below it and skipped it. It is carded now, and how it avoids being a leech is explained below.
2. **لَام الأَمْر after وَ or فَـ takes a sukūn, not a kasrah.** Your notes say "if there's a wow or faa
   before, it gets a kasra". The rule runs the other way: لِيَنْصُرْ, but وَلْيَنْصُرْ and فَلْيَنْصُرْ, as
   in فَلْيَعْبُدُوا رَبَّ هَٰذَا البَيْتِ (106:3). The card (sarf-174) gives the sukūn. Tell me if your
   teacher really said kasrah.
3. **"Only thulāthī mujarrad" for ism al-ẓarf.** That holds for its own patterns (مَفْعَل, مَفْعِل). But
   from a mazīd verb, the noun of time or place still exists; it just takes the ism mafʿūl's form
   (مُجْتَمَع, a meeting place). The other four on that list are right as stated. sarf-191 follows your
   notes for now. Say the word and I'll take ẓarf out of it.

## Decisions you should know about

- **Kāna without a leech.** There's no 13-item list card. The words are grouped by meaning, the way your
  notes group them:
  - **Singles, each with a meaning card in both directions:** كَانَ, صَارَ, مَا دَامَ, لَيْسَ.
  - **The five time verbs** (أَصْبَحَ, أَمْسَى, أَضْحَى, ظَلَّ, بَاتَ): each gets its own meaning card, keyed
    by its time of day. "Became" is the second sense on each, so no two cards share a front.
  - **The four "always, continuously" verbs** share one meaning, so four meaning cards would all have the
    same front, which makes leeches. Instead there's one list cloze (nahw-204) and one card for what they
    mean (nahw-205).
  - **Plus:** the إِعْرَاب effect, a contrast card against إِنَّ (the two families are mirror images, the
    easiest thing to mix up), تَامَّة, كَانَ + مُضَارِع, the only-past pair, and the بِـ on the خَبَر of لَيْسَ.
- **صِفَة now has two senses.** In ṣarf it means a descriptive word; in naḥw it's the describing word in a
  تَوْصِيفِيّ phrase. Your notes draw this line themselves. The ṣarf card (sarf-179) is labelled صَرْف, and
  the old naḥw card (nahw-129) gets a نَحْو label. That label is the one changed naḥw note. The registry is
  updated.
- **One contrast card not in the notes:** الفَاعِل (the doer, a naḥw role, nahw-085) vs اِسْم الفَاعِل
  (a ṣarf noun), sarf-183. Similar names, different things.
- **The amr formation card says "family one".** Your notes give the hamzat al-waṣl step without limits.
  But forms like صَنِّفْ (Form II) take no hamza, and أَكْرِمْ (Form IV) has a hamzat al-qaṭʿ. Every
  family-one verb needs the hamzat al-waṣl.
- **Examples added (not in the notes):** نَاصِر, مَنْصُور, أَكْبَر, كَانَ يَكْتُبُ, لَا تَنْصُرْ / لَا تَنْصُرُ, and
  two verses (39:36 for the بِـ after لَيْسَ, 106:3 for the sukūn). مِفْتَاح is yours.
- **Slips corrected:** "a harf majzoom laam" → a لَام that makes the verb مَجْزُوم (the lām itself is
  جَازِم); "{name of particle}" for kāna → اِسْم كَانَ / خَبَر كَانَ, since kāna and her sisters are
  verbs.

  tagged `oct08`, the day they were found.
