Status: **approved and delivered 2026-11-17.** Every term below is mentioned on a card but has no card of its own.

## New two-way cards (science label: tajwīd on all but hafs-062)

- **hafs-062**: إِعْجام ↔ adding dots to letters that share a written shape
- **hafs-063**: مُحَقَّق ↔ specific (of an articulation point): the sound hits a particular organ
- **hafs-064**: مُقَدَّر ↔ approximate (of an articulation point): the sound hits no particular organ
- **hafs-065**: قَرْع ↔ collision of two parts of the speech organs
- **hafs-066**: قَلْع ↔ separation of two parts of the speech organs
- **hafs-067**: حَرْفا اللِّين ↔ wāw or yāʾ sākinah after a fatḥah, as in خَوْف and بَيْت
- **hafs-068**: المَدّ الطَّبِيعِيّ ↔ the natural madd: a madd letter held for 2 counts
- **hafs-069**: تَقْلِيل ↔ a slight lean, halfway between a plain fatḥah and imālah
- **hafs-070**: إِمالَة ↔ a full lean: the fatḥah toward a kasrah, and the alif after it toward a yāʾ
