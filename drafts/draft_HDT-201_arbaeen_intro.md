## Notes on decomposition

- **حَمْد / شُكْر carry two independent distinctions** — the occasion (favour vs. inherent quality) and the instrument (tongue vs. tongue/heart/limbs). Two contrast notes rather than one, because failing one shouldn't be confusable with failing the other.
- **The three reasons the Sunnah matters** are carded by their content, not by their number. The "name all three" version is Tier 2.
- **The Six Books get one enumeration card, not six.** Individual cards would key on list position, which the deck shuffles away. Reciting the set is the examinable unit here.
- Arabic supplied with tashkīl throughout; the notes are English/transliteration.
