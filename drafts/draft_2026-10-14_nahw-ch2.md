## Check this first

1. **Your CLAUDE note** under المُقَدَّر says "add the categories". They're carded as the words whose iʿrāb is
   مُقَدَّر: the مَقْصُور, the مَنْقُوص, an اِسْم joined to the speaker's ي, and a مُضَارِع ending in a weak letter
   (nahw-220). A second card says which states each اِسْم hides (nahw-221). If you meant the categories of the
   مَبْنِيّ, those are the toggle you've marked "more to be added".
2. **The word لَفْظِيّ:** your notes define it as "state explicitly shown", which would make it the same as
   ظَاهِر. In the usual division, لَفْظِيّ is the iʿrāb of a مُعْرَب word itself, whether shown (ظَاهِر) or
   hidden (مُقَدَّر), and مَحَلِّيّ is the iʿrāb of a مَبْنِيّ word's position. The cards follow that, so the four
   terms don't blur. Check it against the book.

## Corrections to your notes (on the cards only)

- **Where iʿrāb applies:** "always an ism and sometimes faa'il" should read "most isms and sometimes the fiʿl".
  Faa'il is a slip for fiʿl. "Always" clashes with your own list of مَبْنِيّ isms.
- **Bināʾ types:** these are the endings that remain *unchanged*, not "changed".
- **The state جَزْم:** a word in it is مَجْزُوم; the notes repeat "marfoo'".
- **The مَبْنِيّ مُضَارِع:** the list leaves out the مُضَارِع with نُون التَّوْكِيد joined directly, which is مَبْنِيّ
  on fatḥah (nahw-223). You carded that نُون in ṣarf on 10-09.
