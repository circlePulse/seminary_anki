# Draft — naḥw: how the kinds of iʿrāb fit together (2 new notes, 1 edit)

Status: **awaiting approval.**

Source: your Tasheel ch. 2 notes on al-muʿrab wa-l-mabnī, as I revised them in Notion on 2026-10-22.

## What changed in your Notion notes

- **LafDhi** now says the word itself is mu’rab, whether you can hear the ending or not. It used to say
  "state explicitly shown", which is the definition of ẓāhir.
- **Dhaahir and almuqaddar** now sit inside LafDhi as its two kinds, each with an example. They replace the old
  "mu’rab has: dhaahir and ‘almuqaddar" toggle.
- **Mahalli** now says the word is mabniy, with haadhaa as the example.
- **A new toggle** gives the test for telling muqaddar from mahalli.
- **Your CLAUDE note** asking for the categories is replaced by the list of words whose iʿrāb is muqaddar. It's the
  same list as nahw-220.
- **One word dropped:** almuqaddar was "due to some external cause in the form of the word". The cause is the
  word's own form, so "external" is gone.

## Decisions you should know about

- **Your other iʿrāb cards stay as they are.** They were already right. Only nahw-215 changes: "carries on its own
  ending" could be read as "shows on its ending", so it now says "whether you can hear it or not".
- **All Tier 1.**
