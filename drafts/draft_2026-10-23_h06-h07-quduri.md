## Decisions you should know about

- **Merged, not repeated:**
  - Your two notes on reading and practising the Qurʾān (under naṣīḥah, and under the relationships) are one card.
  - "Islam is a complete way of life" appears in both Ḥadīth 6 and 7. It's one card, tagged with both.
  - "A safe distance from the ḥarām" is on the taqwā and waraʿ card.
  - The five relationships are the cloze on the text, since they're the same five.
- **Grouped blanks:** the lists of what naṣīḥah to each one means are clozes. The nine ways to treat others are
  blanked three at a time.
- **One unclear note:** "It is someone's knowledge to ask if they don't know something". The card keeps the clear
  part: ask those who know.
- **Added from what I know:** the Arabic of "no obedience to the creation in disobedience to the Creator"
  (arbh-297), and al-siyāsah al-islāmiyyah in Arabic script.
- **Qudūrī:** the three new terms (rukhṣah, khuff, jawrab) are two-way cards, like the other terms in the deck.
  "3.5 miles" is as in your notes. The agreement over the khuff and the dispute over the jawrab are on the card
  about the jawrab, since a card asking which is which would be a coin toss.
- **Slips fixed:** "Taawa" is taqwā, "inmitible" is inimitable, "parters" is partners, "onness" is oneness, and
  "the wound being cure" is cured.
