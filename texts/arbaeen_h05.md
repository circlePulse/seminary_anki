# Arbaʿīn al-Nawawī — Ḥadīth 5

**Supplied by Claude from the standard text on 2026-10-14, not from the user's copy.** Check it against your
edition. **Reference only: the matn is not to be carded.** The class notes for this ḥadīth come from Notion.

<div dir="rtl">

عَنْ أُمِّ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ أُمِّ عَبْدِ اللهِ عَائِشَةَ رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهَا قَالَتْ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ: "مَنْ أَحْدَثَ فِي أَمْرِنَا هَذَا مَا لَيْسَ مِنْهُ فَهُوَ رَدٌّ".

رَوَاهُ الْبُخَارِيُّ وَمُسْلِمٌ. وَفِي رِوَايَةٍ لِمُسْلِمٍ: "مَنْ عَمِلَ عَمَلًا لَيْسَ عَلَيْهِ أَمْرُنَا فَهُوَ رَدٌّ".

</div>

## Translation

This is the translation in the user's class notes.

> On the authority of the Mother of the Believers, Umm ʿAbd Allāh ʿĀʾishah RA, who said: the Messenger of Allah ﷺ
> said, "He who introduces something new into this matter of ours that is not from it, then it is rejected."
> (Bukhārī and Muslim.) In a narration of Muslim: "Whoever performs an act that our affair is not upon, then it
> is rejected."
